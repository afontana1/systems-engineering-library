# Hypothetical Lab 02 — Tailoring matrix

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 02](../chapters/02.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 2](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **assess project characteristics and tailor the lifecycle, cadence, and controls**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Tailoring decision: classify at least one software parameter, one physical interface, one human procedure and one safety claim.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A dispatch dashboard color can be reversed after a user study; a brake controller design cannot. Divide the work into four decision classes using reversibility and consequence, and assign evidence gates accordingly.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W02; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Treat reversibility and consequence as orthogonal dimensions. High consequence with low reversibility makes prior validation more valuable; low consequence with high reversibility makes inexpensive experiments attractive. A hybrid lifecycle must specify interface points where these work styles exchange evidence.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **tailor the lifecycle to reversibility and consequence**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. A lifecycle is a decision policy, not a religious choice between waterfall and agile. High-uncertainty, reversible configuration choices deserve cheap trials. High-consequence, long-lead hardware and safety decisions need stronger anticipatory evidence. Hybrid development is defensible only when the tailoring boundaries, decision gates, and interfaces are explicit.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Tailoring decision: classify at least one software parameter, one physical interface, one human procedure and one safety claim. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
