# Hypothetical Lab 04 — Decision-rights design

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 04](../chapters/04.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 4](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **design common-mission, self-managing teams with explicit technical leadership and decision rights**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Role test: model who decides a change that simultaneously touches operator experience, API schema and safety fallback.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Northstar gives a cross-functional team authority to change non-safety scheduling rules, while the safety authority approves modifications to accessibility fallback behavior. A routing feature touching both cannot silently inherit the lower approval level.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W04; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

A decision-rights map should record local authority, mandatory consultation, escalation trigger and accountable approval. Independence of teams is conditional on architectural independence of their changes. Review teams that repeatedly block each other as a systems-coupling problem.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **autonomy must have a clear boundary**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Self-management reduces handoff friction when teams possess expertise and decision rights. In a safety-critical sociotechnical system, autonomy means local decisions within explicitly bounded authority, with escalation for cross-system hazards and controlled baselines. Design the RACI, interface owners, and disagreement resolution before delegating.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Role test: model who decides a change that simultaneously touches operator experience, API schema and safety fallback. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
