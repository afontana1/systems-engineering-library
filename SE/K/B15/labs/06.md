# Hypothetical Lab 06 — Outcome hierarchy

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 06](../chapters/06.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 6](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **design for stakeholder value and conduct the midcourse learning and flow review**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Value audit: select two alternatives, name hard constraints, compare two legitimate value weights and state which decisions remain robust.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Alternative A reduces mean wait by 4 minutes but misses the wheelchair service target. Alternative B reduces mean wait by 3 minutes and satisfies access. A weighted score may favor A, but A is infeasible under the stipulated requirement.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W06; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Separate mission need, outcome measure, system behavior and backlog task. A requirement that is a hard gate cannot be converted into a soft score without decision authorization. Consider distributions and worst affected stakeholder groups, not merely an aggregate mean.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **value is an outcome, not the number of stories delivered**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Backlog items are proxies for an operational outcome. Build a value model with accessibility, response time, service coverage and assurance as separate dimensions. Define hard feasibility constraints before adding weighted preferences; otherwise a large gain in convenience can numerically compensate for a safety violation that should not be traded.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Value audit: select two alternatives, name hard constraints, compare two legitimate value weights and state which decisions remain robust. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
