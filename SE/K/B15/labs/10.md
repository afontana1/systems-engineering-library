# Hypothetical Lab 10 — Evidence in CI

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 10](../chapters/10.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 10](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **create a cyber-physical devsecops and continual-integration system, then red-team a failed increment**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Failure drill: fail an HIL timing test, quarantine the build and identify which evidence may still be reused.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A simulated emergency-stop test passes; a delayed sensor update on test hardware violates the stopping response budget. The pipeline must block release on that failure even though the application suite is green.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W10; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Structure the pipeline as model checks→unit tests→integration tests→HIL or environment-qualified tests→review→authorized release. Propagate artifacts and hashes. Where a stage cannot be automated, define a controlled manual gate with clear accountability.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **a cyber-physical pipeline has a physical boundary**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Software tests can be fast, but physical actuators, environmental interactions and certification evidence may need simulation, hardware-in-the-loop and controlled field trials. Assign a test environment and authority to every claim. Define what cannot be updated over the air without operational approval.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Failure drill: fail an HIL timing test, quarantine the build and identify which evidence may still be reused. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
