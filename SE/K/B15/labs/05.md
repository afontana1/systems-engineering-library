# Hypothetical Lab 05 — Hypothesis and stopping rule

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 05](../chapters/05.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 5](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **plan to discover through learning intervals, hypotheses, options, and rolling commitment**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Learning interval: specify an experiment, threshold, maximum allowed exposure and decision branching for pass/ambiguous/fail.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Before purchasing a dispatch server for a 400-request peak hour, Northstar runs load tests at 200, 300 and 450 requests/hour and defines an objective abandonment trigger for the single-server option.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W05; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

An option is not simply work deferred: it is a preserved feasible alternative with an exercise price and expiration conditions. Define what the experiment can reveal, what remains uncertain, and which alternatives are still available after the result.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **planning is sequential reduction of uncertainty**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Replace a false-precision twelve-month feature plan with a sequence of learning commitments. Express each uncertain assumption as a falsifiable hypothesis, choose the cheapest experiment that could disconfirm it, and identify the decision that follows each possible outcome. An option is valuable because it delays irreversible commitment until evidence is sufficient.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Learning interval: specify an experiment, threshold, maximum allowed exposure and decision branching for pass/ambiguous/fail. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
