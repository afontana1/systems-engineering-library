# Hypothetical Lab 08 — Trace query

**Course:** EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 08](../chapters/08.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 8](../../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **integrate descriptive and analytic models, shared knowledge, and a decision-focused digital thread**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Evidence query: after REQ-14 B→C, list impacted components, tests, and approvals and justify each invalidated item.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A change from REQ-14 rev B to rev C affects block BLK-3, service API-2, and tests T-9/T-12. Trace only rev B data and the review falsely concludes evidence is complete.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W08; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Model relationships as typed and versioned edges; a trace to an old requirement revision should be flagged as stale, not merely present. Define change propagation rules and the human role responsible for adjudicating ambiguous traces.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

A defensible answer begins with **model integration is a versioned evidence problem**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. A diagram becomes decision-relevant when its elements link to traceable needs, interface versions, tests and released artifacts. A digital thread must support queries such as which tests become stale after a requirement revision. Treat model provenance and change-impact rules as functional capabilities, not as administrative metadata.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Evidence query: after REQ-14 B→C, list impacted components, tests, and approvals and justify each invalidated item. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
