Hypothetical Lab 12 — Transform as experiment

Course: EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering
Read first: Chapter 12 · Assessed assignment: Original Week 12

Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving present, challenge, revise, and defend the agile systems engineering operating model. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

Practice question: Final pilot charter: produce a falsifiable claim and two ways the organization could discover it is wrong.

Provided evidence and constraints

Recommend piloting WIP limits and integrated tests for 90 days. Success requires median lead time ≤60 days, no growth in critical evidence escapes, and comparable intake mix. Without all three, do not scale.

  • Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W12; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
  • Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
  • You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
  • An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

Investigation procedure

  1. State the decision. Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
  2. Map the mechanism. Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
  3. Calculate or trace a baseline. Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
  4. Stress the logic. Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
  5. Propose a controlled response. Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
  6. Defend the recommendation. Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

Change challenge

The proposal must state baseline, target, leading indicators, lagging outcomes, assurance guardrails, comparison period and stop/go authority. Make the CEO able to distinguish funded investment from assumptions still needing evidence.

What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with a transformation recommendation must be falsifiable, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. A final operating model is a hypothesis about an organization. Specify current performance, mechanisms of improvement, investments, guardrails, pilot duration, evidence requirements, adoption risk, and stop/go decision rights. A CEO recommendation should state what the organization must not do as clearly as what it should.

Stress-case interpretation: Final pilot charter: produce a falsifiable claim and two ways the organization could discover it is wrong. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
