# How to study this book

1. Consult the actual Week N course goals and mastery rubric.
2. Read book Chapter N; reproduce the worked example.
3. Try the questions before reading the explanations.
4. Complete fictional Lab N. Do not copy the reference reasoning into your coursework.
5. Attempt the independently assessed course deliverable and self-review against its original rubric.

The book is a first complete companion, not a peer-reviewed replacement for official standards, specialized texts or expert feedback.
