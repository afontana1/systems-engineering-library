Design and evaluate architecture with quality attributes

Companion chapter: EN.645.764, Week 06 · Phase 1 edition 1.0 (2026-10-30)
Original course: Software Systems Engineering — find Week 6 for assignment, assessment criteria and complete reading plan.
Concepts: quality attribute scenario, availability, performance, architecture tactic

The question to answer

How do vague quality goals become testable architecture decisions?

Conceptual tutorial

Describe a quality under stress

Quality attributes matter in scenarios, not slogans. For availability, define failure stimulus, environment, affected service and recovery measure. For performance, define workload, arrival distribution and percentile latency. For modifiability, define the kind of change and expected effort. Tactics such as redundancy, caching, isolation, retries and circuit breakers have side effects; redundancy may create consistency challenges and retries may amplify overload.

Analyze failure modes and sensitivity points. A service that is '99.9% available' needs a declared measurement window and denominator. If 99.9% is measured over a 30-day month, the simple arithmetic downtime allowance is about 43.2 minutes, though real SLAs specify exclusions, thresholds and accounting rules. A local test cannot establish this field statistic.

Choose evidence for architecture hypotheses

Write a quality attribute scenario and a measurable experiment for each major architecture tactic. Load test a realistic workload; inject failure at a defined point; check time to recovery and user-visible behavior. State what cannot be inferred from the experiment.

Worked application: follow the reasoning

Outage experiment. During a campus network partition, the notification service buffers events for 10 minutes. A test generates 120 updates, drops the link and restores it. Success means no permanent event loss, duplicates handled idempotently and user-visible status marked stale during outage. 'Queue persisted' alone is insufficient.

The misconception to avoid

Watch for: Quality adjectives without measurable stimuli and environments create untestable architecture.
