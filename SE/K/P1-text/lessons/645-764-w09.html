The question to answer

How do you make a credible argument about software dependability?

Conceptual tutorial

Assurance requires a structured claim

A dependability case states a claim ('service avoids duplicate active reservations under specified failures'), reasons and supporting evidence, and assumptions. Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA) enumerates how elements fail and the consequences. Threat analysis enumerates purposeful misuse. These are complementary; a malicious command and an accidental malformed message may share mitigations but require different evidence.

Prioritize by severity, likelihood and detectability only with care: a risk-priority number is not a physical probability. Safety-related decisions must use the governing hazard process and authorized reviewers. Identify independent controls, recovery, observability, supply-chain dependencies and residual exposure. Check what happens after a partial repair and whether operators can recognize degraded state.

Secure development is lifecycle behavior

Dependency pinning, provenance, access reviews, secret handling, vulnerability response and reproducible build records reduce risk. A software bill of materials is an inventory, not proof of security. Follow NIST SSDF as a control framework and connect its practices to concrete review/evidence files.

Worked application: follow the reasoning

Fault tree sketch. Duplicate reservation can require (A) concurrent requests and (B) absent/failed atomic guard, or (C) replay of unrecognized message. Mitigations include transactional uniqueness, idempotency records, reconciliation and alerting. Tests must exercise both concurrency and replay, not simply verify the nominal path.

Practice before viewing solutions

  1. Does an SBOM prove software is secure?
  2. Why is an FMEA RPN not a probability?
  3. Name two independent duplicate-reservation controls.

<details> <summary><strong>Show worked answers</strong></summary>

  1. Does an SBOM prove software is secure? No, it inventories components; vulnerabilities, build integrity and correct operation still need evidence.
  2. Why is an FMEA RPN not a probability? Severity, occurrence and detectability ordinal rankings are often multiplied as prioritization heuristics, not statistically calibrated likelihoods.
  3. Name two independent duplicate-reservation controls. Atomic constraint/version check and persisted idempotency token, plus reconciliation.

</details>

The misconception to avoid

Watch for: Equating extensive risk spreadsheets with validated controls gives false confidence.
