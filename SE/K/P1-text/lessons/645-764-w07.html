The question to answer

What breaks when locally correct modules become networked services?

Conceptual tutorial

Distribution changes the semantics

A local method call can often be assumed to occur in one process. A remote request can be delayed, duplicated, reordered or lost; timeouts do not distinguish a failed action from a successful action whose reply was lost. Design for at-least-once delivery with idempotent handling when exactly-once effects cannot be guaranteed by the end-to-end design. Correlation IDs and versioned state aid reconciliation.

Choose consistency policy deliberately. Strong coordination can prevent conflicting reservations at the cost of availability under partition; eventual consistency improves decoupling but can expose transiently divergent views. Identify which invariants demand one authoritative transactional boundary and which projections may lag. Document failure domains, capacity, geography and deployment ownership.

Configuration is part of architecture

Feature flags, service endpoints, secrets, schema versions and retry settings are software behavior. Version them with review and tests. A configurable architecture that lacks compatibility checks can fail in more ways than a fixed one.

Worked application: follow the reasoning

Duplicate delivery. Broker redelivers AssignVehicle(trip=17, vehicle=4, token=abc) after an acknowledgement timeout. A handler checks whether token abc was committed and returns the same decision rather than reserving another slot. If the payload changes under the same token, it rejects the conflict.

Practice before viewing solutions

  1. Why does a timeout not prove failure?
  2. What is a suitable idempotency-key policy?
  3. When might eventual consistency be acceptable?

<details> <summary><strong>Show worked answers</strong></summary>

  1. Why does a timeout not prove failure? The operation may have succeeded but the response was lost.
  2. What is a suitable idempotency-key policy? Bind token to operation identity and payload hash, persist outcome with business state, return same outcome on replay, reject mismatched payload.
  3. When might eventual consistency be acceptable? For nonauthoritative rider status projections if stale data is clearly labeled and critical reservations remain controlled.

</details>

The misconception to avoid

Watch for: Network calls are not reliable local function calls; retrying blindly causes duplicate effects.
