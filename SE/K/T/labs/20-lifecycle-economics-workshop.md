# Practice Lab T20 — Engineering economics: lifecycle costs, options, and organizational value

## Scenario
A municipal authority is comparing two fleet options with different upfront costs, maintenance obligations, and service performance.

## Investigation
1. Download [lifecycle-options.csv](lifecycle-options.csv) and calculate the annual undiscounted costs.
2. Use [lifecycle.py](../tools/lifecycle.py) to compute the present value of a three-year lifecycle with the specified rate and end-of-year payments.
3. Determine the annual operating-cost reduction required to justify a 200-unit acquisition premium.
4. Vary the discount rate between 0%, 5%, and 10%. Explain how the ranking could change over a longer horizon or with a terminal value.
5. Add two mandatory nonfinancial criteria and propose how the decision board will record them.

## Deliverable
Write a one-page options appraisal with cash-flow assumptions, sensitivity results, hard constraints, and a clear statement of whose costs and benefits are represented.
