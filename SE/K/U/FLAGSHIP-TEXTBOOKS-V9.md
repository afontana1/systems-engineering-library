# v9 flagship textbooks — in-depth companion modules

**Start here:** [Open unified encyclopedia](OPEN-ENCYCLOPEDIA.html), select **Textbook-depth chapters**, then read in sequence.

1. [F01 — From mission need to defensible assurance](flagships/F01-requirements-to-assurance.md) — requirements, traceability, claims and release envelopes; [evidence notebook](flagship-notebooks/01-evidence-graph.ipynb).
2. [F02 — Architecture decisions](flagships/F02-decisions-and-architecture.md) — preference modeling, dominance, Bayesian decision analysis; [decision notebook](flagship-notebooks/02-trade-decisions.ipynb).
3. [F03 — Statistical assurance](flagships/F03-statistical-assurance.md) — exact confidence bounds, dependence, reliability and model validity; [statistics notebook](flagship-notebooks/03-statistical-assurance.ipynb).
4. [F04 — Dynamics and resilience](flagships/F04-dynamics-and-resilience.md) — conservation, queueing, numerical solution, feedback and service recovery; [dynamics notebook](flagship-notebooks/04-dynamics-queues.ipynb).

The series has **38 worked problems**, four Graphviz/SVG diagrams, four standard-library labs and notebooks, and four editable CSV worksheets. All numerical scenarios are fictional teaching inputs. Passing tests verifies code examples, not safety claims or real-world suitability.

## Paths and formats

- Editable source: `flagships/*.md` and `flagship-diagrams/*.dot`.
- Standalone offline reader: `OPEN-ENCYCLOPEDIA.html` (reading and search are self-contained; notebooks and worksheets require the extracted ZIP).
- Executable Python tools: `flagship-labs/`; Jupyter notebooks: `flagship-notebooks/`; datasets: `flagship-worksheets/`.
- Prior v8 materials, all 240 weekly chapters and all 20 original guides are preserved.

## Review limitations

These are newly written teaching treatments with independently unit-tested calculation examples. They are **not** exhaustive textbooks, peer-reviewed safety guidance, externally validated models, or a substitute for applicable standards and professional judgment. The original guides' mastery gates remain in force.
