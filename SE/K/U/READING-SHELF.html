Authoritative reference shelf and reading map

The encyclopedia works offline, but these sources require internet access unless you obtain copies separately. Links are not endorsements, and each source must be interpreted for its scope and revision.

  • NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, Rev 2 (NASA/SP-2016-6105): lifecycle, system design, verification, validation, technical management. Useful with concept dossiers 01, 02, 03, 06 and 07.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge (SEBoK): systems concepts, engineered systems, enterprise and complex-system perspectives; helpful throughout the atlas.
  • NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods: measurement processes, experiment design, statistical inference, reliability and modeling. Start with 07 and 08.
  • NASA Technical Reports Server – NASA Systems Engineering Handbook: versioned technical-report copy of the handbook.
  • NIST SP 800-160 Volume 1: engineering trustworthy secure systems; use with 03, 04, 10 and 11.

Suggested source-reading protocol

  1. Begin with a concrete question from the original weekly guide and its corresponding dossier.
  2. Locate the authoritative section that discusses the method or boundary condition; record the document and revision.
  3. Reproduce one quantitative or procedural example independently.
  4. Compare the source assumptions with your case. Explain why any mismatch matters.
  5. Add a citation to the design, test plan or decision record; do not cite a general handbook as evidence that a specific design is adequate.
