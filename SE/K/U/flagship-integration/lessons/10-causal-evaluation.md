### D10 extension · A difference-in-differences result, with its missing assumption

Let treated mean wait change from 22 to 13 minutes, while a comparison group changes from 20 to 18. Difference-in-differences is $\Delta=(13-22)-(18-20)=-9-(-2)=-7$ minutes. This estimator is a causal effect only under defensible counterfactual assumptions, especially parallel untreated trends, no differential concurrent events, and an appropriate definition of interference. A three-minute system-wide average improvement could hide harm to a rural or accessible subgroup.

**Exercise and solution.** If comparison-group after-wait were 14, its change would be $-6$ and the DiD estimate would be $-3$ minutes. The fitted comparison is not evidence that the groups would have evolved in parallel without intervention. [Case data](../flagship-integration/data/ns-u4.1-case.json).
