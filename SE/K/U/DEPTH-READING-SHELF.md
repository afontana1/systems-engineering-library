Link verification performed for the v8 release (2026-10-31). Specific standards, editions and policies can evolve; verify current applicability before engineering use.

[NASA reference](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) · [NASA technical report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/20170001761.pdf).
2. **SEBoK (Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge).** Cross-cutting concepts and references; consulted v2.14 release notice dated 18 May 2026. [SEBoK home](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Guide_to_the_Systems_Engineering_Body_of_Knowledge_%28SEBoK%29).
3. **NIST SP 800-160 Volume 1 Revision 1: Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems (2022).** Systems-security-engineering principles and trustworthiness through lifecycle activities. [NIST publication](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v1/r1/final).

**Use as a critical reader.** For every quantitative claim distinguish mathematical assumptions from operational evidence, and record the publication/version that supports any normative interpretation.
