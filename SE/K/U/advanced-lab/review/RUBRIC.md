# Scoring guide (100 points)

| Area | Points | Demonstrated evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope and assumptions | 12 | Explicit system boundary, exclusions and stakeholder use |
| Architecture semantics | 15 | Graph links and independently argued contracts |
| Software invariants | 15 | Positive and deliberately negative tests; correct status accounting |
| Fault injection | 15 | Reproducible scenarios, detection assumptions and credible failure explanations |
| Experiment design | 15 | Paired runs, clear estimands, CI interpretation, sensitivity plan |
| Traceability and provenance | 15 | Claim→requirement→test→scenario→output with hashes |
| Decision quality | 13 | Decision limits, defeaters, risk owner and ethical concerns |

**Minimum:** 80/100 for strong laboratory competence, *plus* an explicit “not field-ready” boundary. No numerical score confers certification.
