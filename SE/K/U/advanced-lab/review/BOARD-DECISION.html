Advanced laboratory — reference review board disposition

Decision: approve continued educational use under synthetic laboratory conditions; do not authorize field deployment, safety assurance or real dispatch.

Evidence reviewed

  • Structural checks of architecture.json passed but only establish model graph consistency.
  • 29 executable tests cover fault visibility, interval conflicts, idempotency in sequential processing, scenarios, and provenance packaging; they do not cover concurrency or physical behavior.
  • The baseline fixture completes 12/12 unique requests with nearest-rank p90 wait = 20 min. The outage fixture completes 6/12, message-loss fixture 8/12, delay fixture 10/12, telemetry block fixture 4/12 and one-vehicle fixture 6/12. These numbers are deliberately fixed teaching inputs.
  • The independent-seed paired experiment compares completion under the same synthetic demand streams. Its confidence band covers simulation precision only.
  • The provenance manifest records hashes, not validation authority.

Defeaters / unresolved issues

  1. No geography, vehicle movement, boarding, accessibility, real customer preferences or service equity.
  2. Lost requests are known to the experimental truth ledger, not to a feasible operational monitor.
  3. No concurrent request processing, durable database or adversarial security model.
  4. Simplified telemetry and downtime intervals; no physical repair model or human authority.
  5. Demand is generated by an uncalibrated exponential interarrival distribution and identical trip lengths.
  6. Fixed targets were not elicited from real stakeholders and are not operational acceptance criteria.

Required before any expanded use

Produce a model credibility plan; involve domain and human-factors experts; collect lawful representative data; validate critical failure modes; establish cyber and privacy controls; audit independent evidence; and define accountable authorization. This is a proposed future plan, not a completed approval.
