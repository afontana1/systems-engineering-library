# v11.0 integrated engineering laboratory

New integrated laboratory: ten editable instructional chapters, one connected fictional case, reusable CSV requirements/interfaces/hazard/decision data, standard-library Python scheduler and evidence auditor, 25 automated tests, scenario runner with reproducibility hashes, four instructional notebooks, editable diagrams and a fully self-contained offline study website. All v10 materials are retained in the complete master archive.

## Limits of release

* The implementation is **single-process, batch, synthetic and educational**. It does not contact vehicles or networks.
* No statement of field reliability, operational safety, accessibility, cybersecurity or real-world effectiveness is established by this package.
* Fixed fixture success, synthetic-arrival simulations and trace-link coverage are deliberately separated from independent validation and release approval.
