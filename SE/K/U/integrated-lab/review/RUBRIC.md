# Integrated lab assessment rubric (100 points)

| Category | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Mission framing and system boundary | 10 | SOI, external actors, exclusions, stakeholder validation plan and meaningful MOEs |
| Requirements and bidirectional traceability | 15 | Testable, sourced requirements; correct method and explicit acceptance oracles; meaningful evidence paths |
| Architecture and interface reasoning | 10 | Dataflow, model contracts, invariants, failure modes, coherent change impact |
| Trade study and design decisions | 10 | Hard constraints before weighted preferences, alternative scenarios, sensitivity and assumptions |
| Executable prototype and tests | 15 | Reproducible run, negative/boundary tests, explicit limitations, invariant coverage |
| Modeling and uncertainty | 10 | Replications, sensible uncertainty calculations, sensitivity, credibility and model-vs-field distinction |
| Hazards and human factors | 10 | Serious residual risks, plausible control actions, accessibility and human-review plan |
| Configuration and evidence provenance | 10 | Reproducible inputs, hashes/version, results and stale-evidence analysis |
| Integrated review and communication | 10 | Honest disposition; open findings; actionable conditions for next review |

**Gate rule:** A submission cannot earn a high overall competency rating if it fails to identify the synthetic-model boundary or asserts real operational safety from these fixtures, regardless of point totals. Use a second reviewer when possible; these scores are not a university grade.

**Self-review protocol:** (1) Attempt deliverables before opening reference responses; (2) reproduce tests; (3) deliberately alter one assumption; (4) document what claim becomes unsupported; (5) explain one finding you would not close.
