Phase 4 authoritative source shelf

These are source pointers for further study, not bundled or reproduced copyrighted textbooks. Some official documents may be revised; check the publication date and current status before applying operationally. Source links are external; the Phase 4 website itself works offline.

Agility and engineering flow

  1. INCOSE Agile Systems & Systems Engineering Working Group — distinguishes agility of products and processes; architecture-enabled response.
  2. INCOSE Systems Engineering Agility Primer — concise framework; access/price may vary.
  3. The Scrum Guide (2020 official) — useful agile framework, not a substitute for physical-system lifecycle, safety and configuration controls.
  4. SEBoK — systems engineering technical and enterprise management context.

Digital and mission engineering

  1. DoD Digital Engineering Strategy (2018) — five strategy goals and implementation focus areas.
  2. DoD Digital Engineering policy and guidance — models, M&S credibility, digital ecosystem, strategy and reference links; confirm version on access.
  3. Mission Engineering resources and guide — official mission-engineering methodology and public guides.
  4. NIST IR 8356 (2025), Security and Trust Considerations for Digital Twin Technology — twin characteristics, security and trust constraints.
  5. OMG SysML specification landing page — formal reference for model language semantics; distinguish SysML versions and tool support.

Process quality, improvement and measurement

  1. ASQ DMAIC process — stages, baseline, measurement, analysis, improvement and control.
  2. ASQ Quality Glossary — reference definitions and statistical vocabulary.
  3. SEBoK Guide — search for enterprise processes, quality management and performance assessment.
  4. NIST Engineering Laboratory — measurement, engineering process and digital-manufacturing research.

Using sources critically

For each source, keep publication/version, statement, intended-use limitation, contradictory evidence and practical consequence. When sources disagree, inspect terminology and scope before choosing. This companion offers explanations and learning examples but does not supersede the original course's required reading and current professional standards.
