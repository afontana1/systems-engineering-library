# Define the lifecycle technical-measurement strategy and decision chain

**Cross-course case:** Fictional Autonomous Campus Shuttle. Figures are synthetic and illustrate reasoning; they do not validate real transport operations.

## The idea in context

A metric is valuable only if it can change a decision. Organize measures into effectiveness, performance, technical progress and risk indicators, with threshold, time window, measurement method, owner and response action. A control chart does not answer whether the service is useful; a stakeholder measure alone may not reveal which subsystem is failing. Build a measure-to-decision map that specifies what intervention follows an amber or red result.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

If 95th-percentile pickup wait exceeds 12 min, the action is to review fleet utilization and scheduling. Tracking mean wait alone is insufficient because rare long waits could violate the mission objective even while the average improves.

**Assumption to challenge:** Choosing easy-to-count telemetry rather than measures with explicit decisions attached. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 1 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. What makes a measure decision-relevant?
2. What is an operational definition?
3. Why is a TPM not automatically a MOE?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **What makes a measure decision-relevant?** It provides information that can change, confirm, time, or bound a named action.

2. **What is an operational definition?** A complete rule for what is measured, for whom or what, when, under which configuration, in what units, and how it is calculated.

3. **Why is a TPM not automatically a MOE?** A TPM is a technical characteristic or margin; a MOE concerns operational or stakeholder effectiveness.

</details>
