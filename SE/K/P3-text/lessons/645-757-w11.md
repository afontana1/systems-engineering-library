# Plan and evaluate verification, validation, credibility, and accreditation for intended use

**Course:** Foundations of Modeling and Simulation in Systems Engineering · **Week:** 11 of 12 · **Phase 3 companion:** edition 1.0  
**Study guide:** [Open the original course guide](../../../C/P3/EN645757.md) and jump to **Week 11** for prescribed readings, full assignment and mastery criteria.  
**Cross-course case:** Fictional Autonomous Campus Shuttle. Figures are synthetic and illustrate reasoning; they do not validate real transport operations.

## The idea in context

Credibility is graded relative to intended use and decision consequence. Verification concerns implementation correctness; validation concerns representation adequacy; accreditation or acceptance is an authority-specific decision to use the model for a purpose. Record evidence quality, domain, configuration, uncertainty and residual model risk. A model can be useful for screening while unfit to certify safety.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

A dispatch queue simulator matches historical mean wait but misses the rainy-day tail. It may be accredited for early fleet screening, not for accessible-service compliance decisions in all weather.

**Assumption to challenge:** Treating a general “validated” label as transferable across scenarios and decision authorities. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 11 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. Can a model be valid in general?
2. What is accreditation?
3. What is conceptual-model validation?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **Can a model be valid in general?** Validation is always relative to intended use, conditions, resolution, and acceptance criteria.

2. **What is accreditation?** An authority decision that an M&S and its evidence are acceptable for a specified use.

3. **What is conceptual-model validation?** Evaluation that the conceptual representation, assumptions, and rules are adequate for intended use.

</details>
