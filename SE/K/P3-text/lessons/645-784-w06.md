## The idea in context

A decision argument should reveal constraints, measurement evidence, weights, uncertainty and rejected options. Visualization must not imply confidence that the underlying evidence lacks. Compare a preferred plan with at least one credible challenger and articulate the condition under which the challenger would become preferable. Decision logs should preserve both process and rationale.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

A preferred three-shuttle fleet is cost-effective under current forecasts but four shuttles win if peak demand rises 30%. A concise chart shows this switching region alongside the cost of adding capacity later.

**Assumption to challenge:** Presenting only a winning alternative and hiding the thresholds that reverse the recommendation. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 6 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. Why show value profiles?
2. What is a decision record?
3. Why freeze dashboard exports?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **Why show value profiles?** To reveal where alternatives gain and lose value across objectives rather than only showing totals.

2. **What is a decision record?** A controlled account of authority, alternatives, evidence, assumptions, rationale, dissent, decision, and revisit triggers.

3. **Why freeze dashboard exports?** To preserve exactly what the decision authority saw.

</details>
