# Specify physical architectures, variants, configurations, instances, and lifecycle baselines

**Course:** Applied Analytics for Model-Based Systems Engineering · **Week:** 10 of 12 · **Phase 3 companion:** edition 1.0  
**Study guide:** [Open the original course guide](../../../C/P3/EN645632.md) and jump to **Week 10** for prescribed readings, full assignment and mastery criteria.  
**Cross-course case:** Fictional Autonomous Campus Shuttle. Figures are synthetic and illustrate reasoning; they do not validate real transport operations.

## The idea in context

Physical architecture instantiates technologies and configurations that realize logical responsibilities. Separate type from instance: three vehicles are instances of a vehicle type, and software variant 2 is not interchangeable with variant 1 without evidence. Baselines must bind requirements, interfaces, simulation parameters and test artifacts to a precise configuration. Configuration effectivity matters when hardware and software evolve at different rates.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

A charger model calibrated for a 40-kWh battery cannot silently support a 55-kWh replacement. Record vehicle instance, pack variant, charger rating, control version and the applicable charging curve before rerunning cycle-time claims.

**Assumption to challenge:** Comparing outputs from different physical configurations as if only one parameter changed. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 10 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. What is a variant?
2. What is an instance?
3. What is configuration applicability?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **What is a variant?** A valid alternative form of a common system or element defined by controlled variation.

2. **What is an instance?** A particular realized or planned individual with identity and values based on one or more types/configurations.

3. **What is configuration applicability?** The condition under which an element, requirement, interface, analysis, or V&V item applies to a configuration.

</details>
