## The idea in context

Structural validity checks each causal mechanism, conservation law and parameter range; behavioral validity compares reference modes, extreme tests and withheld data. A model can reproduce history through compensating parameter errors, so calibration is not proof of causality. Report what the model explains, what it does not and how robust its policy ranking is.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

Two backlog models both fit historical peak size. One assumes demand seasonality; the other assumes delayed fleet repairs. A maintenance intervention separates their predictions and is a stronger model test than fit alone.

**Assumption to challenge:** Treating curve-fitting as proof of feedback structure or extrapolating beyond calibration regimes. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 8 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. What is structure validity?
2. What is behavior validity?
3. Why is fit insufficient?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **What is structure validity?** Evidence that modeled relationships and decision rules are acceptable representations for the purpose.

2. **What is behavior validity?** Evidence that the model reproduces relevant patterns and responses.

3. **Why is fit insufficient?** Different structures can fit the same data and still yield different policies.

</details>
