# Develop the M&S plan and conceptual model before implementation

**Course:** Foundations of Modeling and Simulation in Systems Engineering · **Week:** 02 of 12 · **Phase 3 companion:** edition 1.0  
**Study guide:** [Open the original course guide](../../../C/P3/EN645757.md) and jump to **Week 2** for prescribed readings, full assignment and mastery criteria.  
**Cross-course case:** Fictional Autonomous Campus Shuttle. Figures are synthetic and illustrate reasoning; they do not validate real transport operations.

## The idea in context

A conceptual model is the bridge between a real problem and code. Define entities, activities, state, event logic, resources, assumptions, input-output boundaries and the observation plan before implementation. Build process flow diagrams and invariants that can be tested independently. The conceptual model should be reviewed by someone who understands the operation, not just the program.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

A queueing model explicitly decides whether passengers abandon after 15 minutes, whether a vehicle can carry two mobility-device users and whether recharging interrupts a route. These are not coding details: they determine the service result.

**Assumption to challenge:** Adding operational rules in code without updating the conceptual model or stakeholder approval. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

## Test understanding before reading the answers

The next three questions are adapted from the **original guide's Week 2 knowledge check**, with its answer guidance retained so you can learn and self-check in one place.

1. What is a conceptual model?
2. Why trace a measure to model elements?
3. What is termination logic?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **What is a conceptual model?** A reviewable specification of the relevant real-world concepts, relationships, rules, assumptions, and measures represented by the executable model.

2. **Why trace a measure to model elements?** To show how the model produces evidence and to support change-impact and verification.

3. **What is termination logic?** The rule defining when a run or entity process ends and how final state is treated.

</details>
