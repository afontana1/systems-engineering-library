## The idea in context

Environments are active scenario processes, not static temperature labels. Wind, precipitation, road surface and human response may correlate in time and space. Separate observed seasonal structure, short-term stochastic variation and rare extremes. Scenario validity matters more than visual realism: an elaborate weather animation with independent random variables can be less useful than a simple joint empirical model.

## Worked reasoning: apply it to the shuttle case

Rain increases both rider demand and braking distance. Sampling those independently understates concurrent system stress. Use a shared weather-regime variable and conditional distributions.

**Assumption to challenge:** Using independent scalar environment perturbations when the mission has common-cause stressors. Record what observation or test would expose this failure before using the result in a decision.

2. What is a scenario taxonomy?
3. Why validate autocorrelation?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal guided answers</strong></summary>

1. **Why model environment dynamically?** Conditions evolve, correlate, trigger events, and interact with system states.

2. **What is a scenario taxonomy?** A controlled classification of nominal, observed, extreme, fault, and future cases.

3. **Why validate autocorrelation?** A correct marginal distribution can still have unrealistic temporal sequences.
