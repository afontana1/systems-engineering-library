Resource shelf — open and authoritative starting points

These are links to other authors' material, not copies. Availability, accessibility and licenses can change. Prefer the Atlas article for first-pass intuition, then use these for more detailed reading and confirmation. Formal specifications are reference texts, not beginner tutorials.

Phase 0: Foundations and management

  • NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — Reference chapters for lifecycle, needs, requirements, design, technical management, integration and V&V.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
  • GAO Cost Estimating and Assessment Guide — Program cost estimation, uncertainty, assumptions and management.

Phase 1: MBSE and software-intensive systems

  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
  • OMG SysML formal specifications — Precise notation and semantics; consult when you need authoritative modeling constructs; not a beginner textbook.
  • OMG UML formal specification — Formal language definition for software structure, interaction and behavior models.
  • CMU SEI: Early Analysis of Software Architecture — Detailed quality-attribute and architectural reasoning studies.
  • NIST SP 800-160 Rev. 1 — Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems — Security and trustworthiness integrated with engineering lifecycle reasoning.

Phase 2: Concept, design, integration and test

  • NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — Reference chapters for lifecycle, needs, requirements, design, technical management, integration and V&V.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
  • NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.
  • NIST SP 800-160 Rev. 1 — Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems — Security and trustworthiness integrated with engineering lifecycle reasoning.

Phase 3: Analytics, modeling, simulation and decisions

  • NASA-STD-7009B: Standard for Models and Simulations — Modeling intended use, V&V, qualification, credibility and limitations.
  • NASA-HDBK-7009B: Models and Simulations Implementation Guide — Companion explanatory handbook to the NASA modeling standard.
  • NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.
  • MIT System Dynamics Self Study / Road Maps — Free exercises, readings, and self-study material on feedback and dynamic systems.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.

Phase 4: Agile, digital and process engineering

  • U.S. Department of Defense digital engineering and M&S resource portal — Digital engineering strategy, digital artifacts, M&S references and implementation resources.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
  • NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.

Phase 5: Systems of systems, enterprises and complexity

  • SEBoK — Systems of Systems (SoS) — Synthesis of constituent autonomy, SoS architecture and capability engineering.
  • SEBoK — Enterprise Systems Engineering — Enterprise structures, value, governance and transformation.
  • MIT System Dynamics Self Study / Road Maps — Free exercises, readings, and self-study material on feedback and dynamic systems.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.

Reading protocol

  1. Start with the course map and choose the next week.
  2. Read the linked Atlas primers before the instructor notes.
  3. Work through the original guide's examples and your own reproduction.
  4. Open exactly one or two external references to resolve a definition, derivation, or method.
  5. Produce the original week's artifact; log unclear terms for future encyclopedia expansion.

The list is curated, not an endorsement that every reference is freely downloadable or pedagogically self-contained.
