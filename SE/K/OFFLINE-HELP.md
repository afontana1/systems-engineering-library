# Reading and Study Help

1. Extract the complete library archive into a folder. Do not open pages from within the ZIP.
2. Open the root `index.html` in your browser.
3. Use **Concepts**, **Subject Guides**, **Courses**, **Textbooks**, or **Labs** in the global navigation. The **Encyclopedia** is also available from that bar.
4. You can bookmark any concept, chapter, or textbook page directly.

The site does not save reading progress, study markers, or checkpoints. Core educational content is bundled in the archive; selected external references require internet access.

For navigation help, see [Keyboard and accessibility help](ACCESSIBILITY-HELP.html).
