Primary reference shelf for Phase 2

Sources below are established references for deeper learning and verification of terminology. They are not all beginner textbooks. Links require internet access, while all original chapters and the offline site remain self-contained.

  • NASA Systems Engineering Handbook — systems design, product realization and technical management
  • NASA Handbook Appendix — requirements quality, verification matrices and integration planning
  • NASA Technical Assessment — technical reviews and engineering measures
  • SEBoK System Realization — implementation, integration, verification and validation
  • SEBoK System Integration — assembly and integration concepts
  • NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook — DOE, measurement and reliability
  • NIST SP 800-160 Vol 1 Rev 1 — Systems Security Engineering

How to use the references

  • Conceptual design: study NASA system design processes and technical assessment alongside the first 12 chapters; use NIST when uncertainty and experiments drive choice.
  • Design and integration: use NASA's integration-plan outline and SEBoK system integration as comparison checklists; examine security assurance claims with NIST SP 800-160 where applicable.
  • Test and evaluation: use NASA's verification/validation method descriptions and NIST's measurement, experimental design and reliability sections; distinguish standards-based methods from these synthetic teaching exercises.

No proprietary standard or copyrighted textbook is reproduced. All chapter explanation is original pedagogical synthesis. The authoritative sources may have application limits and should be consulted directly for actual programs.
