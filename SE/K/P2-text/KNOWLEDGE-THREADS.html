Concept pathways across Phase 2

Each path crosses weekly lessons in the order that an engineering claim develops. These complement the richer original guide assessments.

Mission evidence → concept baseline

Move from observations to mission need and approved concept claims.

  • EN.645.767 W01 — Reopen the problem and audit the incumbent baseline
  • EN.645.767 W02 — Stakeholder evidence and current-state analysis
  • EN.645.767 W03 — Desired end state, ConOps, scenarios, and mission calculator v0.1
  • EN.645.767 W04 — Conceptual requirements, use cases, gaps, and experiment planning
  • EN.645.767 W12 — Final concept baseline, oral defense, and handoff

Concept choices → physical responsibility

Follow a functional exchange into a controlled requirement allocation and interface.

  • EN.645.767 W05 — Functional architecture and mission-to-function traceability
  • EN.645.767 W06 — Timing, interfaces, allocations, and Functional Architecture Review
  • EN.645.767 W07 — Generate physical concept families with morphological and set-based methods
  • EN.645.768 W02 — Close the system specification
  • EN.645.768 W03 — Allocate requirements and define component functions
  • EN.645.768 W04 — Mature the physical architecture, interfaces, and performance budgets

Uncertainty → experiment → review

Quantify uncertainty without replacing stakeholder decisions with solver output.

  • EN.645.767 W08 — Resource, affordability, schedule, feasibility, and optimization models
  • EN.645.767 W09 — Objectives, value functions, and multiattribute evaluation
  • EN.645.767 W10 — DOE, parametric analysis, uncertainty, sensitivity, and Down-select Review
  • EN.645.767 W11 — Risk, opportunity, affordability, schedule, and Concept Validation Review
  • EN.645.768 W05 — Use prototypes and experiments to retire technology and design risk
  • EN.645.769 W04 — Apply measurement science, statistics, DOE, and sample rationale

Technical budgets → integration evidence

Go from performance allowances to testable interfaces and fault evidence.

  • EN.645.767 W06 — Timing, interfaces, allocations, and Functional Architecture Review
  • EN.645.768 W04 — Mature the physical architecture, interfaces, and performance budgets
  • EN.645.768 W06 — Integrate multidisciplinary design and conduct the PDR-style review
  • EN.645.768 W10 — Plan system integration and conduct the Integration Readiness Review
  • EN.645.769 W05 — Construct the integration test approach, SIL/HIL paths, and informal tests
  • EN.645.769 W07 — Integrate hardware, software, cybersecurity, automation, and test-tool evidence

Dependability → measured assurance

Separate architecture estimates, failure mechanisms and statistical evidence.

  • EN.645.768 W07 — Design for reliability, availability, and maintainability
  • EN.645.768 W08 — Design for human performance, safety, security, usability, and resilience
  • EN.645.768 W09 — Design for supportability, producibility, supply, sustainability, and disposal
  • EN.645.769 W04 — Apply measurement science, statistics, DOE, and sample rationale
  • EN.645.769 W08 — Plan environmental, quality, reliability, maintainability, safety, and acceptance testing
  • EN.645.769 W10 — Analyze results, determine evidence sufficiency, and recommend remedial action

Planned evidence → bounded readiness

Connect the design's testability to performed evidence and final authority.

  • EN.645.768 W11 — Baseline verification, validation, customer evidence, and CDR readiness
  • EN.645.768 W12 — Conduct the Final Design & Integration Review and hand off to T&E
  • EN.645.769 W01 — Receive the design baseline and create the integrated T&E strategy
  • EN.645.769 W02 — Trace requirements and stakeholder expectations to objectives, methods, and critical test parameters
  • EN.645.769 W06 — Write formal qualification procedures and conduct the Test Readiness Review
  • EN.645.769 W09 — Execute tests and control data, deviations, discrepancies, and corrective action
  • EN.645.769 W12 — Conduct the final evidence review and make the operational-readiness recommendation
