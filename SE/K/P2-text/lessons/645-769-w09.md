# Execute tests and control data, deviations, discrepancies, and corrective action

**Companion chapter:** EN.645.769 · Week 09 · Phase 2 edition 1.0 (2026-10-30)  
**Original study guide:** [System Test & Evaluation](../../../C/P2/EN645769.md) — consult **Week 9** for the complete assignment, original source readings and mastery rubric.  
**Concepts:** test execution, discrepancy report, deviation control, corrective action  
**Case:** Fictional Autonomous Campus Shuttle, dispatch and transport service; all figures are illustrative, not deployment guidance.

## The engineering question

> How can you preserve the truth when the actual test diverges from the procedure?

## Conceptual tutorial

### Execution produces records, not just verdicts
A run record should capture the procedure revision, operator, date/time, article build, configuration, environment, calibration state, commands, raw measurements, anomaly observations and stop/restart decisions. Collect data in a controlled manner before performing interpretations. Maintain a trace from each observation to the test step and applicable threshold.

Differentiate a **deviation** (departure from approved procedure or setup), **discrepancy** (observed mismatch against expectation), **nonconformance** (verified failure against a requirement or specification) and **test-system fault** (problem in the evidence apparatus). Investigate cause before assigning disposition. Corrective action should change the underlying cause and include a verification-of-fix test, not simply rerun until a pass is observed.

### Repetition requires a rule
Decide when a test is invalid, when it is a real failure, and whether a retest needs approval. Preserve failed runs and changes. If a run must be excluded, record the criterion and evidence explaining why; silent deletion produces a misleading record.

## Worked example: follow the reasoning

**Deviation scenario.** A scripted run reports 1,100 ms latency against 900 ms acceptance. Investigation shows the test harness logged timestamps on unsynchronized machines with 250 ms drift. Record the discrepancy and calibration evidence, classify the test measurement as invalid for that criterion after authorized review, correct synchronization, then rerun under the controlled procedure. Do not rewrite the original file to make it look passing.

## Retrieval questions — attempt these first

1. What is the difference between a failed device and an invalid test?
2. Why is "rerun until pass" unacceptable?
3. What is required to close a corrective action?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal explained answers</strong></summary>

1. **What is the difference between a failed device and an invalid test?** The former violates the specified behavior; the latter cannot reliably measure the behavior due to setup/tool/process defect.
2. **Why is "rerun until pass" unacceptable?** It hides failure probability and may select only favorable observations.
3. **What is required to close a corrective action?** Cause analysis, controlled correction, evidence of effectiveness and authorized disposition.

</details>

## Critical misconception

**Watch for:** Treating an uninvestigated discrepancy as a test-system error biases the program toward passing.
