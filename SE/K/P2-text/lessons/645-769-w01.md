## The engineering question

> How do you design testing around a decision rather than around available equipment?

## Conceptual tutorial

### Evaluation precedes test selection
A T&E strategy starts with what decision must be made, who has authority, the relevant baseline and what evidence would change the decision. Different activities answer different questions: development tests reduce engineering uncertainty; formal verification establishes compliance against requirements; validation evaluates suitability for intended use; acceptance may be performed by or for the customer under agreed criteria. These categories may share resources but cannot be collapsed into a single "system passed" label.

List test objectives, critical parameters, candidate methods, article types, facilities, schedule, resource constraints and safety rules. Use a test pyramid or staged build-up to gather cheaper, more diagnostic evidence first, then representative integration and operational evidence. Define the risks of relying on simulation, especially model credibility and untested physical or human interfaces.

### Preserve independence and traceability
The group producing evidence should expose limitations and discrepancies, not simply confirm design team's optimistic expectations. Capture design version, configuration, test article pedigree, execution environment and authorized review criteria. An evidence plan must address both requirements-level verification and scenario-level validation.

## Worked example: follow the reasoning

**Evidence ladder.** For rider notification performance, start with component timing profiling, then service-integration load tests, then network-fault tests, and finally a controlled rider-workflow evaluation. A passing component timing test is useful development evidence, but it cannot establish a 99th-percentile end-to-end claim under field-equivalent load or validate rider understanding.

## Retrieval questions — attempt these first

1. What question should a T&E strategy state first?
2. Why is simulation evidence conditional?
3. How do acceptance and development tests differ?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal explained answers</strong></summary>

1. **What question should a T&E strategy state first?** The decision, authority, baseline and evidence needed for an informed disposition.
2. **Why is simulation evidence conditional?** Model structure, parameter validity and environment representativeness limit what simulation results can support.
3. **How do acceptance and development tests differ?** Development tests diagnose and reduce uncertainty; acceptance evaluates agreed delivery criteria for authorized stakeholders.

</details>

## Critical misconception

**Watch for:** Letting the availability of a test rig define the entire evidence program confuses convenience with adequacy.
