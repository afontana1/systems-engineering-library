## The engineering question

> What new evidence is needed when a system enters an operational ecosystem?

## Conceptual tutorial

### The environment has independent actors
Operational use introduces changing riders, infrastructure, organizations, policies and other systems. In a system of systems, constituent services may have independent authority and release cycles, creating interface and governance risk. Validation must include real work practices, exceptions, accessibility, maintainability and operator control within approved conditions. A lab configuration rarely captures the full causal structure of field performance.

AI/autonomy components require additional attention to data provenance, model version, operational design domain, edge cases, distribution shift, fallback, human oversight and monitoring. A model accurate on a held-out dataset may still fail under different lighting, weather, population or sensor degradation. Define what the system is authorized to do and how it detects conditions outside that domain.

### Deployment should be a bounded hypothesis
Start with restricted trials and a monitoring plan approved for safety and privacy. Predetermine stop/go triggers and who may invoke them. Separate the dispatch teaching example from actual driving control: operational vehicle safety requires domain-specific independent assurance, legal authority and professional review.

## Worked example: follow the reasoning

**Distribution-shift case.** A route-estimation model trained on weekday demand performs well in validation data but is deployed on a campus-event weekend with atypical arrival bursts. Median wait increases sharply. This is not automatically a software defect; it may be a domain shift. Evaluate the model's operational assumptions, monitoring alerts and reversion policy before expanding its scope.

## Retrieval questions — attempt these first

1. Why is operational evaluation different from lab verification?
2. Why does high held-out accuracy not guarantee field suitability?
3. What should a deployment stop criterion include?

<details><summary><strong>Reveal explained answers</strong></summary>

1. **Why is operational evaluation different from lab verification?** Operational context includes independent systems, human behavior, constraints and changes absent in the lab.
2. **Why does high held-out accuracy not guarantee field suitability?** Training and validation distributions may not represent deployment conditions or rare hazards.
3. **What should a deployment stop criterion include?** Observable trigger, measurement, decision authority, safe action and recovery conditions.

</details>

## Critical misconception

**Watch for:** Treating autonomy accuracy as a replacement for safety and operational suitability is a false equivalence.
