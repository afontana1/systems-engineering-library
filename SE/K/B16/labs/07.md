# Hypothetical Lab 07 — Authority federation

**Course:** EN.645.782 — Foundations of Digital and Mission Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 07](../chapters/07.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 7](../../../C/P4/EN645782.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **collaborative digital-engineering ecosystem and asot governance**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Construct an authority table for five artifacts belonging to the city, fleet operator and software supplier.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A contractor owns vehicle firmware configuration while the agency owns operational route rules. A mission view can combine them only by identifying exact version pairs and access limitations.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W07; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Authority can be distributed across data owners. A federated query must record the source owner, released revision and access restrictions for every resolved element. Avoid false agreement created by synchronized copies of incompatible sources.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

A defensible answer begins with **authority and access are part of model architecture**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. An authoritative source of truth is a governed responsibility, not a single giant database. Distinguish who may originate a fact, who may approve it, and who may reuse it. Track data sensitivity, identity, change history, pedigree, and archival policy across federation boundaries.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Construct an authority table for five artifacts belonging to the city, fleet operator and software supplier. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
