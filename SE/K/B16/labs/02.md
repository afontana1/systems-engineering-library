# Hypothetical Lab 02 — Competency and governance

**Course:** EN.645.782 — Foundations of Digital and Mission Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 02](../chapters/02.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 2](../../../C/P4/EN645782.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **digital engineering strategy, policy, competencies, and adoption**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Prioritize three changes within a constrained six-month improvement budget and state the adoption and proof metrics.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A modeling team has mature diagram tools but releases conflicting interface versions. Prioritize configuration reconciliation and signed baselines before procuring a more elaborate graphical editor.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W02; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Map a specific failure to capability: poor versioning needs configuration practice, missing hazard reasoning needs systems expertise, and weak access control needs security architecture. Acquiring a software license is not evidence of any of these.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **strategy is governance plus capability**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Adopting tools does not itself establish a trustworthy digital environment. A strategy must allocate authority for model ownership, access, interfaces, version control, competence, and validation. Assess organizational capability with a concrete workflow and a measured failure—not only staff certifications or licenses.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Prioritize three changes within a constrained six-month improvement budget and state the adoption and proof metrics. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
