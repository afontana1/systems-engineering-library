# Hypothetical Lab 12 — Reproducible evidence package

**Course:** EN.645.782 — Foundations of Digital and Mission Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 12](../chapters/12.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 12](../../../C/P4/EN645782.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **integrated digital engineering for mission decisions**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Produce one end-to-end evidence chain and a change-impact query that breaks when any reference revision changes.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Demonstrate one mission decision using explicit model release IDs, demand scenario, software version, verification record, confidence bound and accountable approving role. Leave unresolved claims marked open.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W12; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

A defensible decision must be rebuildable from versions of data, model, scenario, software, verification and approval. The final review should exhibit a query, a counterexample and an explicitly unclosed claim.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **a digital mission strategy is an evidence portfolio**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. The final release should show what decision each digital asset enables, the model’s intended use, authoritative owners, trace queries, security controls, scenario evidence and known limitations. A strategy is credible when an external reviewer can reproduce a bounded decision from a controlled baseline.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Produce one end-to-end evidence chain and a change-impact query that breaks when any reference revision changes. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
