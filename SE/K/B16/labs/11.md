# Hypothetical Lab 11 — Alternative mission interventions

**Course:** EN.645.782 — Foundations of Digital and Mission Engineering  
**Read first:** [Chapter 11](../chapters/11.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 11](../../../C/P4/EN645782.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **mission analysis, alternatives, gaps, and interventions**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Compare mutual-aid and vehicle-purchase strategies using capacity, timing, access and coordination constraints.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Compare purchasing two vehicles against a mutual-aid agreement plus improved prepositioning. Test whether accessibility, response-time and staffing requirements are satisfied under the same disruption scenarios.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W11; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Model nonproduct interventions as real options with costs and effects. Evaluate all candidates against the same scenarios and hard requirements. Document distributional impacts; a mean performance improvement can reduce service to high-need groups.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **choose interventions across the mission, not just the product**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. Mission alternatives can include procedures, training, mutual aid, information quality, deployment policy and hardware. Use scenario analysis to expose gaps and select interventions through trade studies with hard constraints, sensitivity and uncertainty.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Compare mutual-aid and vehicle-purchase strategies using capacity, timing, access and coordination constraints. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
