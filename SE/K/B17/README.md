## NS-PI17.1 controlled process improvement laboratory

The twelve chapters share a [Northstar change-to-evidence case](case/INDEX.html) with [explicit synthetic assumptions](case/controlled-inputs.json), [96 generated requests](case/synthetic-requests.csv), a [standard-library reproducible simulation](tools/step03_book17_process.py), a [separately sourced Phase 4 conformance sample](case/shared-lab-event-violations.csv), [controlled policy results](case/single-run-policy-results.csv), [factorial experiments](case/factorial-results.csv), [stress results](case/arrival-stress-results.csv), and an [assurance/risk register](case/assurance-and-risk-register.csv). The invented teaching sample does **not** reconstruct the 96-item course history; the model is not a formal process appraisal or a deployment qualification. All twelve original chapters and laboratories remain in place.
