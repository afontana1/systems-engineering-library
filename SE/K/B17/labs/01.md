# Hypothetical Lab 01 — Multiple quality dimensions

**Course:** EN.645.783 — Systems Engineering Process Improvement  
**Read first:** [Chapter 01](../chapters/01.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 1](../../../C/P4/EN645783.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **continuous improvement and quality in systems engineering**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Write a problem charter with two outcome metrics, two guardrails and a well-defined scope.

## Provided evidence and constraints

Current median change lead time is 34 days, p90 is 81 days, and 27% of items are reworked after review. A target of 20 days is meaningful only if critical escapes do not increase.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W01; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Separate process time, decision quality, customer outcome, escape rate and human burden. An intervention is an improvement only relative to an objective with explicit guardrails; it may not optimize all dimensions simultaneously.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **improve outcomes, not paperwork speed**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. A process produces technical decisions and evidence, not just completed forms. Establish mission outcomes, quality escapes, elapsed lead time, cost of rework and human impacts before picking interventions. A faster review that omits hazard evidence is degradation, not improvement.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Write a problem charter with two outcome metrics, two guardrails and a well-defined scope. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
