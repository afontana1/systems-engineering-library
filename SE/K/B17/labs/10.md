# Hypothetical Lab 10 — Control versus specification

**Course:** EN.645.783 — Systems Engineering Process Improvement  
**Read first:** [Chapter 10](../chapters/10.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 10](../../../C/P4/EN645783.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **process control, governance, tailoring, and sustainment**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Define owner responses to one process outlier and one trend that breaches a stakeholder target without breaching control limits.

## Provided evidence and constraints

A weekly chart shows three unusually high rework rates following a supplier change. The owner investigates intake conditions and technical cause rather than forcing teams to meet the old target by reclassifying defects.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W10; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

Control limits are inferred from process variation under assumptions; specification limits come from requirements. An apparently controlled but unacceptable process still needs improvement.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **sustainment requires feedback and decision ownership**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. A control plan identifies process owners, sampling intervals, definitions, thresholds and response actions. Statistical control charts detect process signals only under their model assumptions. Governance should update the process when evidence changes without losing baseline accountability.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Define owner responses to one process outlier and one trend that breaches a stakeholder target without breaching control limits. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
