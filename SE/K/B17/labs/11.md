# Hypothetical Lab 11 — Threats to generalization

**Course:** EN.645.783 — Systems Engineering Process Improvement  
**Read first:** [Chapter 11](../chapters/11.md) · **Assessed assignment:** [Original Week 11](../../../C/P4/EN645783.md)

## Situation and objective

Northstar's fictional program review committee has received a proposed change involving **independent red team, trio evaluation, and scale decision**. The program lead wants an immediate favorable recommendation, the safety lead requires an intact evidence chain, and the operator demands a measurable benefit. Your task is to avoid a purely procedural response and build a bounded, reviewable engineering decision.

**Practice question:** Draft a red-team memo that separates verified findings, plausible concerns and requested evidence.

## Provided evidence and constraints

An intervention shortens median lead time 25%, but accessibility escapes rise from zero to two in the pilot. Recommend a pause and root-cause investigation, not an unconditional rollout.

- Configuration: instructional Northstar baseline P4-W11; all observations are synthetic and must not be presented as measured field evidence.
- Required guardrails: traceability to the released requirement revision; safety and security exceptions must be reviewed; inaccessible demand cannot be discarded to inflate a service metric.
- You have authority to recommend an experiment, not to bypass a release gate or certify the system.
- An alternative explanation must be recorded and actively tested rather than dismissed.

## Investigation procedure

1. **State the decision.** Name the decision maker, available options, consequence of being wrong, and deadline. Define a testable success claim.
2. **Map the mechanism.** Diagram the causal chain or model relationships described in the chapter. Record inputs, units, release identifiers, and who owns each assumption.
3. **Calculate or trace a baseline.** Reproduce the supplied nominal result. Distinguish direct observation, derived value, and illustrative assumption.
4. **Stress the logic.** Apply the adverse change below. Note which constraints become active, which evidence becomes stale and which stakeholders are affected.
5. **Propose a controlled response.** Specify the smallest legitimate experiment, evaluation criteria, independent reviewer, and stop rule.
6. **Defend the recommendation.** Present one argument in favor and one credible counterargument. Explicitly identify evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

### Change challenge

An independent reviewer attacks selection effects, unseen rework, staff overtime, small sample size, compliance drift and unintended distributional consequences. Do not ignore an adverse guardrail to protect the pilot result.

## What to submit

Create a one- to three-page decision note (Markdown is fine) containing a decision table, the key calculation or trace, assumptions, uncertainty, configuration, guardrail status and next evidence-gathering action. Include one diagram or dependency map and a compact evidence index. Do not copy the reference critique as your answer.

### Reference critique — view only after attempting

<details><summary>Expand reference reasoning</summary>

A defensible answer begins with **an independent red team should try to disprove success**, not with a preferred tool or ceremony. The reviewer asks whether improvement is real, transferable, safe and sustainable. Check measurement bias, unmeasured harms, confounders, scalability and evidence quality. A scale decision can be limited or conditional, with named corrective actions and re-review dates.

**Stress-case interpretation:** Draft a red-team memo that separates verified findings, plausible concerns and requested evidence. An unqualified approval would be inappropriate if critical evidence, stakeholder impact, or configuration ownership remains unresolved. A conditional decision can be valid when it specifies the next test, independent reviewer and stop rule. Compare your reasoning against this structure and revise your memo where the evidence chain is incomplete.
