### Study routine

1. Open the corresponding original weekly guide and note its reading tasks, assessed deliverable and mastery gate.
2. Read this chapter and reproduce the independent worked example with a changed assumption.
3. Attempt the five knowledge checks without revealing answers. Then explain any errors in your own words.
4. Follow the deeper essay and use a CSV or Python lab when a quantitative or relational method is involved.
5. Build the original guide assignment. Preserve the assumptions, configuration, alternative perspectives, reviewer challenges and evidence limitations.
