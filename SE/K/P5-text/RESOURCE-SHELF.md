# Phase 5 authoritative reference shelf

These links point to authoritative resources for further study. **The publications are not reproduced** in this library. Some external references require access, and source versions may change; record version/date and applicability when using them for decisions.

## Systems of systems

1. [SEBoK: Systems of Systems](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Systems_of_Systems_%28SoS%29) — independence, emergence and SoS classification; inspect definitions and caveats.
2. [US DoD Systems Engineering Guide for Systems of Systems (2008)](https://ac.cto.mil/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/SE-Guide-for-SoS.pdf) — technical and management processes applied under SoS authority and evolution constraints.
3. [US defense research and engineering policy / guidance hub](https://www.cto.mil/sea/pg/) — current and historical SoS references; check document status before use.
4. [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) — core system design, realization and technical management methods; adapt rather than equate product SE with SoS governance.

## Enterprise systems and dynamics

5. [SEBoK main guide](https://sebokwiki.org/) — look for Enterprise Systems Engineering, The Enterprise as a System, Enterprise Architecture and Systems Thinking.
6. [The Open Group TOGAF reference](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf) — enterprise architecture viewpoints and governance; access and exact edition may vary.
7. [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) — for cross-lifecycle assurance and architecture context.

## Complexity, critical systems and method selection

8. [SEBoK Systems Thinking](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Systems_Thinking) — open systems, perspectives, complexity and systems approaches.
9. [SEBoK Complexity](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Complexity) — characteristics and limits of complex systems modeling.
10. [Open University Systems Thinking resources](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/systems-thinking-and-practice) — introductory systems thinking study (availability may vary).
11. Jackson, *Systems Thinking: Creative Holism for Managers* and *Critical Systems Thinking and the Management of Complexity* — bibliographic pointers for critical systems methodologies, **not bundled**.
12. Checkland, *Systems Thinking, Systems Practice* — bibliographic pointer for Soft Systems Methodology, **not bundled**.
13. Ulrich, *Critical Heuristics of Social Planning* — bibliographic pointer for critical boundary judgments, **not bundled**.

## How to evaluate a source

Write down the source's intended purpose, actual claim, publication year or revision, evidence base, assumptions, contradictory sources, affected stakeholders, and the decision for which the claim might be used. Historical guidance is not necessarily current policy or law. External links require internet access; all companion lessons work offline.
