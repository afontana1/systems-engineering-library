# Companion map: EN.645.767 — System Conceptual Design

**Phase:** 2 — Concept, design, integration and test  
**Original learning guide:** [phase-2/en-645-767-system-conceptual-design.md](../../C/P2/EN645767.md)

## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Stakeholders, needs, and value](../encyclopedia/stakeholders-and-needs.md) — A stakeholder is anyone with a legitimate interest in, effect on, or consequence from a system.
- [Concept of operations and scenarios](../encyclopedia/conops-and-scenarios.md) — A concept of operations (ConOps) explains how a proposed capability will be used in its operational environment, from the perspective of people and organizations.
- [Functional, logical, and physical architectures](../encyclopedia/functional-vs-physical.md) — Functional architecture describes what transformations and behaviors must occur, independent of which hardware or software will perform them.
- [Trade studies, constraints, and sensitivity](../encyclopedia/engineering-trades.md) — A trade study compares feasible alternatives against agreed objectives, costs, risks and constraints.
- [Measures of effectiveness and performance](../encyclopedia/metrics-hierarchy.md) — A measure of effectiveness (MOE) describes mission or stakeholder outcome; a measure of performance (MOP) describes a technical or operational behavior that contributes to that outcome.
- [Requirements and acceptance criteria](../encyclopedia/good-requirements.md) — A requirement is a controlled statement of a necessary system obligation.

## Direct concept-selection trade method

Use [Pareto and robust design decisions](../methods/pareto-and-robust-design.md) to distinguish hard constraints from stakeholder preferences and calculate a documented dominance relationship across multiple architectural alternatives. The method guide includes full scenario-loss and regret calculations; its synthetic results are examples of a method, not decisions for the course's actual concept baseline.

## Best supporting shelf for this course

- [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) — Reference chapters for lifecycle, needs, requirements, design, technical management, integration and V&V.
- [Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Guide_to_the_Systems_Engineering_Body_of_Knowledge_(SEBoK)) — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
- [NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/nistsematech-engineering-statistics-handbook) — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.
- [NIST SP 800-160 Rev. 1 — Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v1/r1/final) — Security and trustworthiness integrated with engineering lifecycle reasoning.
