Knowledge lenses for this course

  • Work breakdown structures and technical baselines — A work breakdown structure (WBS) decomposes total project scope into manageable, deliverable-oriented work packages.
  • Risk, issue, uncertainty, and opportunity — A risk is an uncertain future event or condition that could affect objectives; an issue is a condition already present that requires action.
  • Trade studies, constraints, and sensitivity — A trade study compares feasible alternatives against agreed objectives, costs, risks and constraints.
  • Measures of effectiveness and performance — A measure of effectiveness (MOE) describes mission or stakeholder outcome; a measure of performance (MOP) describes a technical or operational behavior that contributes to that outcome.
  • Agile systems engineering and lifecycle tailoring — Agile systems engineering shortens learning cycles and exposes assumptions through increments, while preserving the safety, interface and evidence obligations of engineered systems.
  • Flow, work in process, and bottlenecks — Work in process (WIP) is work started but not finished.
