Knowledge lenses for this course

  • Digital thread and digital twin — A digital thread is the traceable flow of linked engineering information across lifecycle decisions and artifacts: needs, models, designs, tests, configurations and field observations.
  • Mission threads and capability analysis — Mission engineering starts with mission outcomes and analyzes what capabilities, interactions and dependencies must be coordinated to achieve them.
  • Model, method, language, tool, and repository — A model is a purposeful representation of a system for answering questions.
  • Simulation verification, validation, and credibility — Simulation verification asks whether the implementation represents the conceptual model correctly; simulation validation asks whether that model adequately represents reality for its intended use.
  • System of systems and constituent independence — A system of systems (SoS) combines constituent systems to achieve capabilities none delivers alone.
  • Requirements traceability and impact analysis — Traceability is a justified network of relationships among needs, requirements, functions, design elements, tests, risks and decisions.
