# Companion map: EN.645.757 — Foundations of Modeling and Simulation in Systems Engineering

**Phase:** 3 — Analytics, modeling, simulation and decisions

## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Conceptual modeling and intended use](../encyclopedia/conceptual-m-and-s.md) — A conceptual model identifies the entities, relationships, assumptions, mechanisms, variables, temporal scope and output claims of an analysis before implementation.
- [Probability, distributions, and dependence](../encyclopedia/probability-and-dependence.md) — An uncertain quantity is not defined by its mean alone.
- [Discrete-event simulation and queues](../encyclopedia/discrete-event-simulation.md) — A discrete-event simulation (DES) represents a system as events that change state at particular times: an arrival, service start, breakdown, or repair.
- [Simulation verification, validation, and credibility](../encyclopedia/model-vv-and-credibility.md) — Simulation verification asks whether the implementation represents the conceptual model correctly; simulation validation asks whether that model adequately represents reality for its intended use.
- [Design of experiments and sensitivity](../encyclopedia/design-of-experiments.md) — Design of experiments (DOE) chooses combinations of input factors so their influence on outcomes can be distinguished efficiently.
- [Stocks, flows, and feedback loops](../encyclopedia/stocks-flows-feedback.md) — A stock is an accumulated state, such as work backlog or installed fleet; a flow changes it per unit time.

## Phase 5 technical reading: Input calibration and rare-event analysis

Add the [likelihood and importance-sampling reference](../methods/simulation-calibration-and-rare-events.md) to output interpretation and credibility. The guide distinguishes a fitted Poisson rate, held-out model checks, Monte Carlo tail error and model-form uncertainty; it does not assert a real service failure probability.

## Best supporting shelf for this course

- [NASA-STD-7009B: Standard for Models and Simulations](https://standards.nasa.gov/node/263) — Modeling intended use, V&V, qualification, credibility and limitations.
- [NASA-HDBK-7009B: Models and Simulations Implementation Guide](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-HDBK-7009) — Companion explanatory handbook to the NASA modeling standard.
- [NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook](https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/nistsematech-engineering-statistics-handbook) — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.
- [MIT System Dynamics Self Study / Road Maps](https://learn.mit.edu/?resource=4010&resource_title=introduction-to-system-dynamics) — Free exercises, readings, and self-study material on feedback and dynamic systems.
- [Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Guide_to_the_Systems_Engineering_Body_of_Knowledge_(SEBoK)) — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
