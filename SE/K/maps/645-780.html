Knowledge lenses for this course

  • Agile systems engineering and lifecycle tailoring — Agile systems engineering shortens learning cycles and exposes assumptions through increments, while preserving the safety, interface and evidence obligations of engineered systems.
  • Flow, work in process, and bottlenecks — Work in process (WIP) is work started but not finished.
  • CI/CD, configuration, and software assurance — A reliable software delivery pipeline transforms controlled source into repeatable build artifacts and test evidence.
  • Process capability and continuous improvement — Process improvement treats engineering work as a system with customers, inputs, activities, outputs, variation, measures and feedback.
  • Work breakdown structures and technical baselines — A work breakdown structure (WBS) decomposes total project scope into manageable, deliverable-oriented work packages.
  • Integration build-up and readiness evidence — Integration is the progressive assembly of elements into a functioning system while checking their interactions.
