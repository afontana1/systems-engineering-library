Knowledge lenses for this course

  • System of systems and constituent independence — A system of systems (SoS) combines constituent systems to achieve capabilities none delivers alone.
  • Authority, governance, and coordination — Governance defines who may decide, commit resources, approve changes, resolve conflicts and accept risk.
  • Mission threads and capability analysis — Mission engineering starts with mission outcomes and analyzes what capabilities, interactions and dependencies must be coordinated to achieve them.
  • Interface control and integration contracts — An interface control definition specifies what crosses a system-element boundary and under what rules.
  • Resilience, robustness, and graceful degradation — Robustness means maintaining acceptable performance across disturbances or uncertainty; resilience includes preparing for, absorbing, recovering from and adapting after disruptions.
  • Complexity, emergence, and adaptation — Complexity arises when interactions, feedback, heterogeneous actors, history and adaptation make whole-system outcomes hard to infer from component properties.
