# Companion map: EN.645.742 — Management of Complex Systems

**Phase:** 5 — Systems of systems, enterprises and complexity  
**Original learning guide:** [phase-5/en-645-742-management-of-complex-systems.md](../../C/P5/EN645742.md)

## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Complexity, emergence, and adaptation](../encyclopedia/complexity-emergence.md) — Complexity arises when interactions, feedback, heterogeneous actors, history and adaptation make whole-system outcomes hard to infer from component properties.
- [The enterprise as a system](../encyclopedia/enterprise-as-system.md) — An enterprise combines people, policies, information, assets, relationships and capabilities to pursue evolving purposes.
- [Resilience, robustness, and graceful degradation](../encyclopedia/resilience-adaptability.md) — Robustness means maintaining acceptable performance across disturbances or uncertainty; resilience includes preparing for, absorbing, recovering from and adapting after disruptions.
- [Authority, governance, and coordination](../encyclopedia/authority-and-governance.md) — Governance defines who may decide, commit resources, approve changes, resolve conflicts and accept risk.
- [Interventions, policy resistance, and learning loops](../encyclopedia/intervention-and-learning.md) — An intervention changes conditions in a system to improve outcomes.
- [System of systems and constituent independence](../encyclopedia/sos-characteristics.md) — A system of systems (SoS) combines constituent systems to achieve capabilities none delivers alone.
