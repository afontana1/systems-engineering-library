# Companion map: EN.645.780 — Agile Systems Engineering

**Phase:** 4 — Agile, digital and process engineering  
**Original learning guide:** [phase-4/en-645-780-agile-systems-engineering.md](../../C/P4/EN645780.md)

## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Agile systems engineering and lifecycle tailoring](../encyclopedia/agile-tailoring.md) — Agile systems engineering shortens learning cycles and exposes assumptions through increments, while preserving the safety, interface and evidence obligations of engineered systems.
- [Flow, work in process, and bottlenecks](../encyclopedia/flow-wip-and-queues.md) — Work in process (WIP) is work started but not finished.
- [CI/CD, configuration, and software assurance](../encyclopedia/software-lifecycle-evidence.md) — A reliable software delivery pipeline transforms controlled source into repeatable build artifacts and test evidence.
- [Process capability and continuous improvement](../encyclopedia/process-improvement.md) — Process improvement treats engineering work as a system with customers, inputs, activities, outputs, variation, measures and feedback.
- [Work breakdown structures and technical baselines](../encyclopedia/wbs-and-baselines.md) — A work breakdown structure (WBS) decomposes total project scope into manageable, deliverable-oriented work packages.
- [Integration build-up and readiness evidence](../encyclopedia/integration-strategy.md) — Integration is the progressive assembly of elements into a functioning system while checking their interactions.
