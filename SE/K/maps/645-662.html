Knowledge lenses for this course

  • System of interest and purpose — A system of interest (SoI) is the particular system being analyzed or changed for a specific decision.
  • Boundaries, environment, and interfaces — A boundary allocates what the engineering effort owns or directly controls.
  • Stakeholders, needs, and value — A stakeholder is anyone with a legitimate interest in, effect on, or consequence from a system.
  • Concept of operations and scenarios — A concept of operations (ConOps) explains how a proposed capability will be used in its operational environment, from the perspective of people and organizations.
  • Requirements and acceptance criteria — A requirement is a controlled statement of a necessary system obligation.
  • Verification, validation, and acceptance — Verification asks whether the system or element meets specified requirements; validation asks whether it satisfies intended use and stakeholder needs.
  • Functional, logical, and physical architectures — Functional architecture describes what transformations and behaviors must occur, independent of which hardware or software will perform them.
  • Trade studies, constraints, and sensitivity — A trade study compares feasible alternatives against agreed objectives, costs, risks and constraints.
