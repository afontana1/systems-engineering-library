## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [System of interest and purpose](../encyclopedia/system-of-interest.md) — A system of interest (SoI) is the particular system being analyzed or changed for a specific decision.
- [Boundaries, environment, and interfaces](../encyclopedia/boundaries-and-interfaces.md) — A boundary allocates what the engineering effort owns or directly controls.
- [Stakeholders, needs, and value](../encyclopedia/stakeholders-and-needs.md) — A stakeholder is anyone with a legitimate interest in, effect on, or consequence from a system.
- [Concept of operations and scenarios](../encyclopedia/conops-and-scenarios.md) — A concept of operations (ConOps) explains how a proposed capability will be used in its operational environment, from the perspective of people and organizations.
- [Requirements and acceptance criteria](../encyclopedia/good-requirements.md) — A requirement is a controlled statement of a necessary system obligation.
- [Verification, validation, and acceptance](../encyclopedia/v-and-v.md) — Verification asks whether the system or element meets specified requirements; validation asks whether it satisfies intended use and stakeholder needs.
- [Functional, logical, and physical architectures](../encyclopedia/functional-vs-physical.md) — Functional architecture describes what transformations and behaviors must occur, independent of which hardware or software will perform them.
- [Trade studies, constraints, and sensitivity](../encyclopedia/engineering-trades.md) — A trade study compares feasible alternatives against agreed objectives, costs, risks and constraints.
