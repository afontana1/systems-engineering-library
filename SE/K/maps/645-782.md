## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Digital thread and digital twin](../encyclopedia/digital-thread-and-twin.md) — A digital thread is the traceable flow of linked engineering information across lifecycle decisions and artifacts: needs, models, designs, tests, configurations and field observations.
- [Mission threads and capability analysis](../encyclopedia/mission-engineering.md) — Mission engineering starts with mission outcomes and analyzes what capabilities, interactions and dependencies must be coordinated to achieve them.
- [Model, method, language, tool, and repository](../encyclopedia/model-language-method-tool.md) — A model is a purposeful representation of a system for answering questions.
- [Simulation verification, validation, and credibility](../encyclopedia/model-vv-and-credibility.md) — Simulation verification asks whether the implementation represents the conceptual model correctly; simulation validation asks whether that model adequately represents reality for its intended use.
- [System of systems and constituent independence](../encyclopedia/sos-characteristics.md) — A system of systems (SoS) combines constituent systems to achieve capabilities none delivers alone.
- [Requirements traceability and impact analysis](../encyclopedia/traceability-graph.md) — Traceability is a justified network of relationships among needs, requirements, functions, design elements, tests, risks and decisions.
