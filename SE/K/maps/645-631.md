# Companion map: EN.645.631 — Introduction to Model Based Systems Engineering

**Phase:** 1 — MBSE and software-intensive systems  
**Original learning guide:** [phase-1/en-645-631-introduction-to-model-based-systems-engineering.md](../../C/P1/EN645631.md)

## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Model, method, language, tool, and repository](../encyclopedia/model-language-method-tool.md) — A model is a purposeful representation of a system for answering questions.
- [Architecture views, viewpoints, and consistency](../encyclopedia/views-and-viewpoints.md) — A viewpoint defines conventions and concerns for seeing a system; a view applies those conventions to a particular system.
- [Requirements traceability and impact analysis](../encyclopedia/traceability-graph.md) — Traceability is a justified network of relationships among needs, requirements, functions, design elements, tests, risks and decisions.
- [Stakeholders, needs, and value](../encyclopedia/stakeholders-and-needs.md) — A stakeholder is anyone with a legitimate interest in, effect on, or consequence from a system.
- [Functional, logical, and physical architectures](../encyclopedia/functional-vs-physical.md) — Functional architecture describes what transformations and behaviors must occur, independent of which hardware or software will perform them.
- [Verification cross-reference matrices and test design](../encyclopedia/verification-matrix.md) — A verification cross-reference matrix (VCRM) links each controlled requirement to verification method, level, procedure, success criterion, configuration and evidence.

## Best supporting shelf for this course

- [Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Guide_to_the_Systems_Engineering_Body_of_Knowledge_(SEBoK)) — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
- [OMG SysML formal specifications](https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML) — Precise notation and semantics; consult when you need authoritative modeling constructs; not a beginner textbook.
- [OMG UML formal specification](https://www.omg.org/spec/UML) — Formal language definition for software structure, interaction and behavior models.
- [CMU SEI: Early Analysis of Software Architecture](https://www.sei.cmu.edu/library/early-analysis-of-software-architecture/) — Detailed quality-attribute and architectural reasoning studies.
- [NIST SP 800-160 Rev. 1 — Engineering Trustworthy Secure Systems](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v1/r1/final) — Security and trustworthiness integrated with engineering lifecycle reasoning.
