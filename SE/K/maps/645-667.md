## Knowledge lenses for this course

- [Work breakdown structures and technical baselines](../encyclopedia/wbs-and-baselines.md) — A work breakdown structure (WBS) decomposes total project scope into manageable, deliverable-oriented work packages.
- [Risk, issue, uncertainty, and opportunity](../encyclopedia/risk-vs-issue.md) — A risk is an uncertain future event or condition that could affect objectives; an issue is a condition already present that requires action.
- [Trade studies, constraints, and sensitivity](../encyclopedia/engineering-trades.md) — A trade study compares feasible alternatives against agreed objectives, costs, risks and constraints.
- [Measures of effectiveness and performance](../encyclopedia/metrics-hierarchy.md) — A measure of effectiveness (MOE) describes mission or stakeholder outcome; a measure of performance (MOP) describes a technical or operational behavior that contributes to that outcome.
- [Agile systems engineering and lifecycle tailoring](../encyclopedia/agile-tailoring.md) — Agile systems engineering shortens learning cycles and exposes assumptions through increments, while preserving the safety, interface and evidence obligations of engineered systems.
- [Flow, work in process, and bottlenecks](../encyclopedia/flow-wip-and-queues.md) — Work in process (WIP) is work started but not finished.
