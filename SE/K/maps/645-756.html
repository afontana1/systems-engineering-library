Knowledge lenses for this course

  • Measures of effectiveness and performance — A measure of effectiveness (MOE) describes mission or stakeholder outcome; a measure of performance (MOP) describes a technical or operational behavior that contributes to that outcome.
  • Probability, distributions, and dependence — An uncertain quantity is not defined by its mean alone.
  • Design of experiments and sensitivity — Design of experiments (DOE) chooses combinations of input factors so their influence on outcomes can be distinguished efficiently.
  • Conceptual modeling and intended use — A conceptual model identifies the entities, relationships, assumptions, mechanisms, variables, temporal scope and output claims of an analysis before implementation.
  • Decision uncertainty and value of information — Decision under uncertainty evaluates choices across plausible future states, including their likelihood and consequences.
  • Simulation verification, validation, and credibility — Simulation verification asks whether the implementation represents the conceptual model correctly; simulation validation asks whether that model adequately represents reality for its intended use.

Best supporting shelf for this course

  • NASA-STD-7009B: Standard for Models and Simulations — Modeling intended use, V&V, qualification, credibility and limitations.
  • NASA-HDBK-7009B: Models and Simulations Implementation Guide — Companion explanatory handbook to the NASA modeling standard.
  • NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook — Statistical explanation, worked methods, measurement and design of experiments.
  • MIT System Dynamics Self Study / Road Maps — Free exercises, readings, and self-study material on feedback and dynamic systems.
  • Guide to the Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge — Breadth-first conceptual encyclopedia and pointers to further references.
