# Management of Systems Projects — Book Companion, Volume II

**EN.645.667 — Management of Systems Projects** · 12 book chapters, 12 hypothetical practice laboratories · Complete 12-week companion

The original guide defines the graded engineering work. This volume provides explanatory teaching, worked examples and rehearsal problems, not an alternative accreditation or a substitute for the guide. All monetary and performance data are fictional.

## Learning sequence

1. Read the weekly unit in the [course guide](../../C/P0/EN645667.md).
2. Read its book chapter; independently reproduce calculations and explain assumptions.
3. Complete the paired hypothetical lab before opening the reference response.
4. Use older short primers as optional alternate explanations; complete the original assessed assignment.

## Teaching boundaries

Do not interpret this simulated shuttle proposal as approved design, contract advice, operational safety assurance or real-world project data. The EVM forecasting examples assume particular cost conventions and should not be generalized to every contract.
