## Scenario packet

A Month 5 report has the stated PV/EV/AC data and a supplier six weeks late. The chief engineer reports seven false stops per hour versus an interim threshold of two. All people, figures and institutional decisions are synthetic. Use only the role authority and requirements stated here; if information is missing, list assumptions and propose the evidence needed.

## Student tasks

1. Recompute CV, SV, CPI, SPI and two clearly labeled EAC methods.
2. Write an executive status report that distinguishes cost efficiency from calendar and technical status.
3. Propose two corrective actions and identify evidence required before granting progress credit.
