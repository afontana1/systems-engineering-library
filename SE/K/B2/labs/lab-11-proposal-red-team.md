## Scenario packet

A fictional draft contains conflicting vehicle counts, a missing accessibility test and inconsistent acceptance dates. All people, figures and institutional decisions are synthetic. Use only the role authority and requirements stated here; if information is missing, list assumptions and propose the evidence needed.

## Student tasks

1. Identify at least six factual or contractual inconsistencies with precise source references.
2. Assign severity, correction owner and closure evidence to each finding.
3. Rehearse an oral red-team defense without inventing missing evidence.

## Suggested deliverables

Save `LAB11_DecisionMemo.md` and `LAB11_EvidenceRegister.csv`. The memo should define the decision, alternatives, authority, assumptions, analysis, remaining risks and exit condition. The register should contain claim, source, evidence date, configuration/version and reviewer status. Keep calculations and fact claims separable from judgments.

## Self-review rubric (10 points)

- 2 — Problem and authority framed correctly; 2 — All material scope, schedule, cost and technical effects surfaced; 2 — Reasoning and quantitative work reproducible where applicable; 2 — Counterargument and uncertainty addressed; 2 — Final decision bounded by evidence and approval rights.

## Reference response — read only after attempting

Strong review work blocks unsupported release claims and ties each correction to controlled source files; cosmetic editing alone does not close major inconsistencies. This response is a **minimum argument**, not a unique solution. Full credit requires a traceable rationale and acknowledgement of credible objections. In particular, a model or administrative approval must not be mistaken for a real-world safety authorization.
