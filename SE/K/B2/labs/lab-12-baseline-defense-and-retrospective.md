## Scenario packet

The board asks for a defendable 18-month and $8.4M offer, then presents the adverse Month 5 forecast and one unresolved safety interface. All people, figures and institutional decisions are synthetic. Use only the role authority and requirements stated here; if information is missing, list assumptions and propose the evidence needed.

## Student tasks

1. Assemble a one-page commitment-to-evidence trace and a five-minute executive briefing.
2. Respond to eight fictional board challenges using assumptions and controlled documents.
3. Write a retrospective: three lessons with concrete process changes and owners.

## Suggested deliverables

Save `LAB12_DecisionMemo.md` and `LAB12_EvidenceRegister.csv`. The memo should define the decision, alternatives, authority, assumptions, analysis, remaining risks and exit condition. The register should contain claim, source, evidence date, configuration/version and reviewer status. Keep calculations and fact claims separable from judgments.

## Self-review rubric (10 points)

- 2 — Problem and authority framed correctly; 2 — All material scope, schedule, cost and technical effects surfaced; 2 — Reasoning and quantitative work reproducible where applicable; 2 — Counterargument and uncertainty addressed; 2 — Final decision bounded by evidence and approval rights.

## Reference response — read only after attempting

The final defense must acknowledge forecast deterioration and the unresolved safety claim. A valid decision can authorize continued project definition or restricted work without pretending it authorizes passenger operation. This response is a **minimum argument**, not a unique solution. Full credit requires a traceable rationale and acknowledgement of credible objections. In particular, a model or administrative approval must not be mistaken for a real-world safety authorization.
