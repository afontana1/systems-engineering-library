**Read first:** [Book Chapter 6](../chapters/06-integrated-baseline-review.md). **Then consult:** [Course Week 6](../../../C/P0/EN645667.md).

## Scenario packet

Three project files disagree about a degraded-mode software test; the project manager wants to declare IBR closure. All people, figures and institutional decisions are synthetic. Use only the role authority and requirements stated here; if information is missing, list assumptions and propose the evidence needed.

## Student tasks

1. Build a discrepancy matrix across WBS, schedule, budget and acceptance evidence.
2. Interview two fictional control-account managers using five probing questions.
3. Write a conditional IBR disposition with measurable closure evidence.

## Suggested deliverables

Save `LAB06_DecisionMemo.md` and `LAB06_EvidenceRegister.csv`. The memo should define the decision, alternatives, authority, assumptions, analysis, remaining risks and exit condition. The register should contain claim, source, evidence date, configuration/version and reviewer status. Keep calculations and fact claims separable from judgments.

## Self-review rubric (10 points)

- 2 — Problem and authority framed correctly; 2 — All material scope, schedule, cost and technical effects surfaced; 2 — Reasoning and quantitative work reproducible where applicable; 2 — Counterargument and uncertainty addressed; 2 — Final decision bounded by evidence and approval rights.

## Reference response — read only after attempting

The review should remain open for the degraded-mode scope mismatch. Corrective action requires an accountable owner, revised plan, resource impact, and a demonstrable verification method. This response is a **minimum argument**, not a unique solution. Full credit requires a traceable rationale and acknowledgement of credible objections. In particular, a model or administrative approval must not be mistaken for a real-world safety authorization.
