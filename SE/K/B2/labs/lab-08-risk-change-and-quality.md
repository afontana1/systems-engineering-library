# Hypothetical Lab 8 — The Timeout Change Board

*EN.645.667 · Week 8 · Fictional rehearsal, not a substitute for the course assignment.*

**Read first:** [Book Chapter 8](../chapters/08-risk-change-and-quality.md). **Then consult:** [Course Week 8](../../../C/P0/EN645667.md).

## Scenario packet

A supplier proposes a 500-ms timeout while an existing safety argument assumes a 300-ms bound. All people, figures and institutional decisions are synthetic. Use only the role authority and requirements stated here; if information is missing, list assumptions and propose the evidence needed.

## Student tasks

1. Write a change request mapping technical, cost, schedule, safety, and test impacts.
2. Classify three future risks and two current issues; choose triggers and owners.
3. Conduct a fictional change-control board decision with conditional release criteria.

## Suggested deliverables

Save `LAB08_DecisionMemo.md` and `LAB08_EvidenceRegister.csv`. The memo should define the decision, alternatives, authority, assumptions, analysis, remaining risks and exit condition. The register should contain claim, source, evidence date, configuration/version and reviewer status. Keep calculations and fact claims separable from judgments.

## Self-review rubric (10 points)

- 2 — Problem and authority framed correctly; 2 — All material scope, schedule, cost and technical effects surfaced; 2 — Reasoning and quantitative work reproducible where applicable; 2 — Counterargument and uncertainty addressed; 2 — Final decision bounded by evidence and approval rights.

## Reference response — read only after attempting

Hold the proposed release pending safety and interface reassessment. A board may permit a lab-only test under controls, but it cannot assume nominal performance establishes the degraded-mode safety claim. This response is a **minimum argument**, not a unique solution. Full credit requires a traceable rationale and acknowledgement of credible objections. In particular, a model or administrative approval must not be mistaken for a real-world safety authorization.
