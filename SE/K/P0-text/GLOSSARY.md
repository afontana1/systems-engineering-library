# Phase 0 working glossary

This short glossary distinguishes commonly confused terms. Read linked chapters for decisions, examples and limitations.

**Acceptance.** Customer or delegated authority decision to accept delivery according to the agreed acceptance basis; not identical to validation.

**Architecture.** A representation of system elements and relationships relevant to stakeholder concerns and design decisions.

**Baseline.** An approved reference configuration against which change and performance are assessed.

**CPI.** Cost performance index, EV divided by AC; conditional indicator of cost efficiency.

**ConOps.** Description of how a proposed system is intended to operate across actors, scenarios and environments.

**Control account.** Management control point integrating scope, budget, schedule and responsibility for a WBS element.

**Critical path.** Longest driving activity path under the stated logic/duration constraints; may change with resources and uncertainty.

**Earned value (EV).** Budgeted value of objectively completed authorized work, not actual spending.

**Estimate at completion (EAC).** Current forecast of total final cost; distinct from approved original budget.

**Functional architecture.** Model of required transformations, behaviors and flows independent of physical allocation.

**ICD.** Interface control document specifying agreed responsibilities, formats, timing, errors and tests across a boundary.

**Integrated baseline review.** Review of coherence and executability of scope, cost, schedule, resources, technical content and risks.

**Issue.** Problem or condition that is already occurring, as distinct from an uncertain future risk.

**Measure of effectiveness (MOE).** Metric of operational or mission outcome within a defined context.

**Measure of performance (MOP).** Metric of a system performance attribute contributing to effectiveness.

**PBS.** Product Breakdown Structure: hierarchy of the delivered system elements, not a task list.

**Performance measurement baseline.** Time-phased authorized scope/budget framework against which earned progress is assessed.

**Planned value (PV).** Budget assigned to work scheduled by the status date.

**Requirement.** Controlled statement of a necessary capability, behavior or constraint intended to be satisfiable and verifiable.

**Risk.** Uncertain event or condition with potential positive or negative consequences to objectives.

**SEMP.** Systems Engineering Management Plan, defining how technical engineering is executed and controlled.

**SPI.** Schedule performance index, EV divided by PV; not a direct measure of calendar-time slip.

**Stakeholder need.** Expression of value or problem from an affected perspective, requiring translation into evidence-ready requirements.

**System of interest.** The system chosen as the focus of a defined engineering decision and scope of responsibility.

**Technical performance measure (TPM).** Trended design/performance attribute used to monitor technical maturity and margin during development.

**Validation.** Evidence that a system fulfills intended use and stakeholder needs in relevant conditions.

**Verification.** Evidence that specified requirements have been met by a configured element or system.

**WBS.** Product-oriented breakdown of all authorized project work necessary to deliver scope, supported by a WBS dictionary.
