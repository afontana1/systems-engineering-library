**Suggested editorial method:** For each candidate, establish a narrow definition and exclusions; map at least two textbook chapters; produce a worked example with declared assumptions; identify a versioned authoritative source; connect prerequisites and related concepts; verify Markdown, standalone HTML, and interactive reader navigation; and record review status explicitly. Do not launch all candidate entries solely to increase a completion counter.
